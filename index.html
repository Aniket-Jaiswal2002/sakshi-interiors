What We Do

End-to-end execution across every trade

We handle the complete spectrum of interior and renovation work — one team, one point of accountability, zero compromises on quality.

01

Tile & Stone Installation

Precise laying of ceramic, vitrified, marble, and natural stone surfaces — floors, walls, and wet areas executed with perfect alignment and durable grouting.

02

POP & False Ceiling Work

How We Work

From first briefing to final handover

01

Site Assessment & Scope

We visit the site, review drawings and specifications, and produce a clear scope of work with material quantities, trade sequences, and preliminary timelines — before any commitment is made.

02

Estimation & Agreement

Detailed, itemised estimates with no hidden costs. We align on payment milestones, delivery dates, and scope boundaries so both parties are protected.

03

Mobilisation & Phased Execution

Works begin in the correct trade sequence — civil first, MEP rough-in, false ceilings, tile and woodwork, then finishing coats. Each phase is signed off before the next begins.

04

Quality Checks & Snagging

Internal snagging rounds are conducted by our site supervisor before client walkthroughs. Issues are resolved before they are presented for inspection — not after.

05

Handover & Post-Completion Support

A formal handover with documentation — warranties, material data sheets, and a punch-list sign-off. We remain available for post-completion concerns at no additional charge within the defect liability period.
